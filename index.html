About me

Design grounded in behavior.

My B.A. in Psychology from the University of Washington and UX Design & Research certificate from Caltech inform my approach to research and interaction design.

Working in behavioral health staffing has given me direct exposure to candidate journeys, recruiting operations, placement decisions, scheduling exceptions, and onboarding communications. I bring that domain perspective to the design of more understandable, accessible workflows.
