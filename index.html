My perspective on hiring UX

Clarity creates confidence.

Working with applicants makes me attentive to status, timing, and next steps. These are the questions I would bring to AI-assisted hiring design:

  • Make actions understandable. What happened, why, and what does the person need to do next?
  • Keep people in control. How can someone correct information, question a recommendation, or reach a person?
  • Design for uncertainty. What happens when availability changes, data is incomplete, or a recommendation is low-confidence?
  • Reduce unnecessary effort. Are instructions clear and interactions accessible to someone applying for their first job?

These are design priorities I want to bring to future work, rather than claims of AI product experience.
